Screenshot annotation

Make the point while it is still fresh.

Every screenshot is copied immediately and opens at full resolution. Add a quick mark or turn it into a finished visual without leaving CursorPocket.

  • Mark up precisely. Use pen, highlight, arrows, lines, shapes, text, and numbered steps, with undo and redo.
  • Reshape and protect. Crop, cut, add a flat backdrop, or use solid redaction when the hidden content must not be recoverable.
  • Reuse what is on screen. Read text locally with Windows OCR, sample a colour, focus an area, or pin the image above your work.

Crop, cut, and backdrop create a new capture, so the original stays untouched.

Video recording

See exactly what will be recorded.

Preflight names the source and devices before the countdown. Record a clean screen walkthrough with narration and an optional camera self-view, then save a standard MP4 locally.

  • Set up with confidence. Choose a display, region, or window; confirm the microphone level and camera preview; include the pointer; and select 30 or 60 fps.
  • Stay oriented while live. A red, always-readable HUD shows elapsed time, audio activity, active devices, Stop and save, and Discard.
  • Keep the recording clean. CursorPocket’s command panel and recording HUD stay out of the saved video. Noise suppression and auto-leveling can clean up narration.

Screen, microphone, and camera access begin only after you choose Start recording.

Library

Everything you caught, ready to use.

Open the Library with O to browse every local capture newest first. It is a normal movable, resizable Windows window—not a cloud dashboard.

  • Filter at a glance. Switch between all captures, screenshots, video, audio, text, and links, with a live count for each.
  • Preview without opening another app. Inspect images and text, or play video and audio with seek, volume, speed, repeat, skip, and full-window controls.
  • Act without hunting. Open, mark up, copy a path, reveal the file, or move one or many captures to the Recycle Bin.

Arrow keys navigate, Enter opens, and Space plays or pauses.
