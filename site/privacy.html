Privacy policy · effective August 23, 2026

Your captures are yours.

CursorPocket is a local-first Windows application. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, cloud storage, or content-upload service.

CursorPocket does not transmit screenshots, recordings, audio, selected text, links, settings, file names, or capture history.

Information stored on your PC

CursorPocket writes captures to the folder you select and keeps a local index and settings file under your Windows user profile. Settings include capture preferences, window placement, theme, onboarding completion, and update-check timing.

Screen, microphone, and camera access

Screen capture begins only after you select a screenshot or recording action. The microphone and camera open only for recording modes you enable and close when recording or preview ends. This media is processed locally.

Private update checks

If automatic update checks are enabled, CursorPocket reads https://tanmaysh17.github.io/cursorpocket/update.json at most once every 24 hours. The request sends no installation ID, capture information, settings, analytics event, or custom tracking parameter. GitHub may process ordinary web-server information such as IP address and user agent under its own privacy terms.

You can disable automatic checks in Settings. Manual checks contact the same address only when requested. CursorPocket remains fully usable offline.

Deletion and uninstall

Delete captures through the Library or directly from your capture folder. Uninstalling removes the application but intentionally does not delete your capture files. Local settings can be removed from %LOCALAPPDATA%\CursorPocket.

Diagnostics and support

Crash details may be written locally to help troubleshoot a failure. They are not uploaded automatically. If you open a support issue, include only the information you choose to share and remove private content first.

Questions

Open a privacy or support question in CursorPocket’s GitHub Issues.
